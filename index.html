05 · What this model teaches

UPI (Unified Payments Interface) is an instant payment system developed by NPCI. It connects participating banks and UPI-enabled apps. NPCI operates the payment network; it is not the bank holding every customer's money.

Digital payment systems can make transactions more convenient for customers and merchants, support small businesses, and contribute to the digital economy. Other countries have their own systems, such as Pix in Brazil and PromptPay in Thailand. These are separate national systems, not one shared global payment network.

DEMO ONLY — NO REAL MONEY: This educational webpage is not connected to UPI, NPCI, a bank or a payment app. It does not transfer money, authenticate payments or request a UPI PIN. The QR code only opens this demonstration webpage. Never enter real banking credentials or a UPI PIN.
